# User walkthrough kit (step 23)

This step needs real people. The app is ready for it; the sessions are yours to run.

## Who
2-3 people outside the team, ideally not engineers. Each session about 15 minutes. Do not explain the app first.

## Tasks (read one at a time)
1. "Start a scenario and tell me what you think this is."
2. "Move the project forward until something needs your decision. What is happening?"
3. "Make a choice. What changed? How do you know?"
4. "Finish the project. How did it go and why?"
5. "Find out why your score ended up where it did."
6. "Start over."

## Observe (do not help; note the time stuck)
- Did they notice the disclaimer and the assumptions?
- Did they understand what the four measures mean?
- Did they find **Next event** vs **Advance**?
- Could they tell why a measure moved (feed, notice, run log)?
- Did they find the run log, the replay and the reset?
